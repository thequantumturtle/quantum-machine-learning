# Lab 2 — Quantum Encoding and Similarity

**Duration:** 80 minutes. **Framework:** PennyLane.

Students will compare classical cosine similarity with overlap kernels from angle and amplitude embeddings and estimate overlap with finite-shot sampling. No Qiskit, hardware account, or graded answers in this repository.

Open `lab.ipynb` from JupyterLab after starting Docker. This is an instructor preview until verified.
